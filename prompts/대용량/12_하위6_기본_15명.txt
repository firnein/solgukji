A character sheet of chibi Three Kingdoms (ancient China) characters, super-deformed proportions (about 2.5 to 3 heads tall)
but with mature, characterful faces (not babyish). Each character is drawn inside its own vertical rounded-rectangle panel;
weapons, plumes and banners may stick out above the panel.
Very detailed ink line art with fine hatching, rich watercolor and colored-pencil texture, intricate armor and costume patterns,
warm and consistent style across all characters (same style as the attached reference sheet if one is attached).
EVERY PANEL IS A DIFFERENT ILLUSTRATION, like separate key arts from different stories —— NOT the same photo with a
different person. Never repeat the template "front-facing character, waist up, centered, battlefield with banners behind".
Each panel follows its own SHOT (camera distance, angle, pose) and LIGHT (time, weather, color palette) lines, and its SCENE place.
Panel background colors follow the LIGHT line, NOT the faction color —— neighboring panels must have clearly different
background colors and brightness. Draw a battlefield with banners ONLY when the SCENE is a battle.
Personality first: each face and body language shows who they are (arrogant, gentle, crazed, sly, weary, joyful ...).
FACES MUST BE UNIQUE: never reuse one face —— no generic handsome anime face, no same-face syndrome. Draw each FACE line
exactly and EXAGGERATE it like a caricature (face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, skin, age lines, scars, expression).
Each character must be recognizable at thumbnail size even with the costume covered. Ages really differ (smooth young skin
vs. deep wrinkles), and not everyone is beautiful —— some are ugly, fat, gaunt or brutish.
GENDER: every character is an adult MAN, except the women Diaochan, Da Qiao and Xiao Qiao. Men must read as men at a glance —— a
masculine jaw and brow, a thicker neck, an Adam's apple, broad shoulders, male hairstyles and headwear. Young or handsome men are
young MEN, never girls: no long eyelashes, no blush, no rosy lips, no soft round baby face.
IMPACT BY RANK: MYTHIC and ORNATE characters show their most famous legendary moment from SCENE at full drama —— intense
expression, signature gesture, strong effects (aura, flames, storm, flying debris, light) that may burst out of the panel.
FINE: a recognizable episode with moderate drama. PLAIN: a quieter portrait with a simple hint of the place.
CHARACTER FIRST: these are character cards. The character is always the main subject and fills most of the panel
(at least 2/3 of the panel height, head-to-knees or closer). The face is always visible, large and detailed —— never a tiny
figure in a landscape, never a pure silhouette, never hidden. SHOT changes the angle and pose, NOT the size of the character.
The background is a supporting backdrop behind the character.
Background: flat light green paper (#9BCF88) around the panels. Panels are arranged in a clean, evenly spaced grid with WIDE gaps.
Characters, weapons, capes and banners must NOT overlap or touch the neighboring panels or banners.
Below each panel, a dark green brush-stroke banner with ONLY the panel NUMBER in large white digits (1, 2, 3 ...). No other text,
no names, no title, no frame, no watermark.

TROOP CLASS must be obvious from silhouette, pose and main prop:
SPEARMAN heavy armor, long polearm, planted stance / CAVALRY the rider is the subject, only the horse head or mane peeks in /
ARCHER bow in hand, quiver / ROGUE light armor, agile, short blades / STRATEGIST flowing robe, white feather fan /
SCHOLAR scholar robe and hat, book or tablet, no weapon drawn / SORCERER Taoist robe, staff or talismans, faint glowing magic /
SCHEMER dark robe, high collar, sly, scroll or chess piece.
★ICONIC characters: their famous look and signature weapon come FIRST, the class is only a small hint.
FACTION gives costume colors. LOOK gives each face —— every character must look clearly different (age, face, hair, facial hair, build).
SPLENDOR (rank of the card): PLAIN worn practical gear / FINE polished officer gear, a few gold accents /
ORNATE gold filigree, embroidered silk, flowing cape / MYTHIC gilded armor with dragon or phoenix motifs, golden aura, panel edged in faint gold.
Do not draw the bracket tags, ★ marks or line labels as text.
