A sheet of 4 Three Kingdoms (late Han dynasty China) characters. Calm flat SD role-card illustrations for a mobile card game (the KIIN cards: one irreplaceable role per card).

REFERENCE (attached image): a numbered sheet of the BASE chibi designs of these characters —— character #k in the reference = panel #k here.
Keep each PERSON exactly: the same face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, age, facial hair, hairstyle and headwear, skin tone,
body build, cloth colors and signature weapon or item (see each LOOK, FACE, SIGNATURE and COLORS line).
Take NOTHING else from the reference: its small standing pose, flat light and green blot are replaced by the RANK RULES below.
COLORS give the CLOTH and ARMOR colors only (ignore the word "gold" there) —— the color of metal trim, the light and the background come from the RANK RULES.

RANK: KIIN —— the ROLE card (jade).
A KIIN card is an irreplaceable ROLE, not a legend: the picture shows what this person is FOR. The two things to read at thumbnail size are
the ROLE PROP in the middle of the picture and the one bright jade accent. Everything else is calm, tidy and flat.

RANK RULES (checklist —— every panel must obey all of these):
- CAMERA AND POSE: eye-level, front view or a slight 3/4 turn, HIPS-UP half body, perfectly stable, no motion. The head is top-center, the
  ROLE PROP is the second biggest shape: held at chest height in the center of the picture, large and readable at a glance.
- PROPORTIONS AND LINE: the same chibi family as the reference (about 3 heads tall), clean dark-brown ink outlines, flat soft colors with
  simple two-tone cel shading —— a slightly more polished version of the reference, not more detailed.
- OUTFIT: exactly the base outfit (practical cloth and simple armor, NO gold and NO silver ornaments, no cape unless the base design has one)
  PLUS the jade accent: a jade-green (#2ee6c0) cord, tassel, ribbon or pendant on the outfit, and the key part of the ROLE PROP tinted jade.
- LIGHT: flat, even, soft; low contrast; no rim light, no glow, only a soft contact shadow.
- COLOR: the person's own colors on a muted DUSTY TEAL-GREY background (#5f8a8c). Jade #2ee6c0 is the ONLY bright accent in the picture.
- BACKGROUND: flat dusty teal-grey with a very soft vignette, plus ONE thin flat jade ring (a seal-like circle about 70% of the panel width,
  thin line, centered behind the head and shoulders) that stays completely inside the panel and never touches its edge.
  No scenery, no crowd, no banners.
- EFFECTS: none, except small jade shards, ripples or sparks that belong to the ROLE PROP (see each ROLE line).
- EXPRESSION: calm, professional, focused on the job.
- NEVER: gold or silver trim, dramatic light, scenery, armies, fire or lightning, a tilted dynamic pose, a close-up face.

GAME CROP SAFETY (the game cuts the face out of the top center of each picture for the card and for the small round battle medal):
- The whole HEAD (top of the hair or helmet down to the chin) sits in the TOP-CENTER zone: between 8% and 47% of the panel height, horizontally centered, never off to a side.
- Nothing important within 12% of the left and right edges or in the bottom 20% (the card cuts those). Plumes and flag tips may rise above the head.
- The face is always clearly lit and never covered by a hand, a weapon, a fan, hair or an effect.

EVERY CHARACTER LOOKS DIFFERENT: no two characters on a sheet share the same headgear, hairstyle or facial hair ——
follow each SIGNATURE line exactly (HEAD, FACIAL HAIR, MARK). All panels share the RANK LOOK (that is the point), but each person keeps their own
face, body, headwear and weapon, and their own pose within the rank's pose rule —— never clones in different clothes.
STRATEGISTS AND OFFICIALS ESPECIALLY: never default to "narrow slit eyes + long thin beard + tall black official hat" —— follow each FACE
and SIGNATURE exactly; their eye shapes (round, droopy, bulging, narrow), beards (none, stubble, goatee, short, long) and headwear must differ.
GENDER: every character is an adult MAN, except Diaochan (a woman). Men read as men at a glance —— masculine jaw and brow, thicker neck, broad shoulders, male hairstyles and headwear; no long eyelashes, no blush, no rosy lips, no soft baby face on men.

LAYOUT:
- Image 1024x1536 portrait. ALWAYS a fixed grid of 2 columns x 3 rows = 6 equal slots, reading order left to right, top to bottom.
- Characters 1 to 4 fill the first 4 slots in order.
- The last 2 slots (5, 6) stay EMPTY: plain light green background only, no panel, no banner, no number.
  Do not enlarge or re-space the panels to fill the space —— every slot keeps the same size as on a full sheet.
- Each slot holds ONE tall rounded-rectangle PANEL whose picture runs all the way to the panel edge: no inner border, no frame, no white margin.
- Natural proportions: never stretch, squash or shorten a character to fit a panel.
- Flat light green paper background (#9BCF88) around the panels, NARROW even gaps between them (about 3% of the image width).
  Nothing crosses into a neighbor: no plume, banner, weapon or effect leaves its own panel.
- Under each panel a dark green brush-stroke banner with ONLY its number (1 to 4) in big white digits.
- No other text anywhere: no names, no titles, no captions, no signature, no watermark. Do not draw the line labels as text.

CHARACTERS
1. Zuo Ci  [SORCERER, Independent] —— same person as reference #1
   LOOK: very old, extremely long white beard, blind in one eye (milky eye), slight limp.
   FACE: bony elongated face, one milky blind eye and one sharp mischievous eye, long thin nose, toothless impish grin, deeply wrinkled skin, very long white beard, trickster immortal.
   BUILD: thin very old man, slight limp.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD white rattan crown; FACIAL HAIR extremely long white beard; MARK one milky blind eye, limping on a staff, blue robe, wooden clogs.
   COLORS: his own colors as described (independent).
   ROLE: WARD-BREAKER —— shatters magic barriers so that stratagems land. The picture says it with: a pale glowing barrier lattice of hexagon tiles cracking at the point of a brush, a finger or a talisman.
   POSE AND PROP (the ROLE PROP is the star): leaning on his limping staff, one hand holding a peeled tangerine, a paper talisman stuck to a pale-blue glowing bubble-ward that is cracking, one crane feather floating beside it.
2. Diaochan  [SCHEMER, Independent] —— same person as reference #2
   LOOK: beautiful, elaborate hair ornaments and flowers.
   FACE: delicate oval face, large melancholic almond eyes, small nose, red rosebud lips, porcelain skin, sorrowful seductive beauty.
   BUILD: slender woman.
   SIGNATURE (unique to her on this sheet): HAIR elaborate updo with flowers and dangling hair ornaments; MARK folded fan hiding half her face, long pink silk ribbons.
   COLORS: her own colors as described (independent).
   ROLE: STUNNER —— knocks enemies senseless and breaks their rhythm. The picture says it with: a sharp white impact flash with a few small dizzy stars, something has just been knocked or cut.
   POSE AND PROP (the ROLE PROP is the star): the folded fan raised beside her face and above her open palm two tiny rival warrior figures (like chess pieces) glaring at each other, linked by a thin chain-link ribbon, a few dizzy stars above them.
3. Jia Xu  [SCHEMER, Wei] —— same person as reference #3
   LOOK: old, hunched, thin long mustache, half-closed eyes, wrinkled.
   FACE: wrinkled shrewd old face, half-closed lazy eyes, droopy thin mustache, small mouth, sallow skin, unreadable bored expression.
   BUILD: hunched old man.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD dark hood; FACIAL HAIR droopy thin mustache; MARK hunched back, poison vial.
   COLORS: Wei blue: navy, deep blue and steel with silver.
   ROLE: WEAKENER —— saps the enemy's attack. The picture says it with: something sagging or wilting: a limp drooping flag, a bent blade, a coiled snake —— slack, downward shapes.
   POSE AND PROP (the ROLE PROP is the star): a small poison vial in one hand, the other hand cupped beside his mouth as if whispering, a thin black snake coiled around his forearm; at the bottom edge a limp drooping enemy banner.
4. Xu Sheng  [ARCHER, Wu] —— same person as reference #4
   LOOK: middle-aged, neat mustache, red-plumed helmet.
   FACE: middle-aged steady face, neat mustache, calm watchful eyes, straight nose, composed mouth, reliable officer.
   BUILD: steady medium build.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD plain iron helmet with a red plume; FACIAL HAIR neat mustache; MARK long glaive, bow slung on his back.
   COLORS: Wu red: crimson, red, orange and gold.
   ROLE: BULWARK —— takes the hits for the allies. The picture says it with: a big flat guarding shape (a shield or crossed weapons) planted at the front center, an immovable wide stance.
   POSE AND PROP (the ROLE PROP is the star): a big round wooden shield with a jade rim on his left arm held at chest height in the center, the long glaive planted beside him, a wall of tall river reeds standing behind him (the false wall of reeds on the river), the bow slung on his back.
