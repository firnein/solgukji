A sheet of 4 Three Kingdoms (late Han dynasty China) characters. Refined, cold, silver TRANSCENDENT card illustrations for a mobile card game —— stylized adult anime proportions, not chibi.
FORMAT (strict, see LAYOUT): a 1024x1536 PORTRAIT (taller than wide) image, 2 columns x 2 rows of TALL portrait panels separated by light green gaps, a number banner under each panel.

REFERENCE (attached image): a numbered sheet of the BASE chibi designs of these characters —— character #k in the reference = panel #k here.
Keep each PERSON exactly: the same face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, age, facial hair, hairstyle and headwear, skin tone,
body build, cloth colors and signature weapon or item (see each LOOK, FACE, SIGNATURE and COLORS line).
Take NOTHING else from the reference: its small standing pose, flat light and green blot are replaced by the RANK RULES below.
COLORS give the CLOTH and ARMOR colors only (ignore the word "gold" there) —— the color of metal trim, the light and the background come from the RANK RULES.
If NO image is attached, build each person from the LOOK, FACE, BUILD, SIGNATURE and COLORS lines alone —— they describe the same design.
REALISM LEVEL 2 of 4 (KIIN chibi < TRANSCENDENT < IMMORTAL < MYTHIC): the reference is a chibi cartoon —— do NOT copy its drawing style.
Redraw each person as a NON-CHIBI stylized adult: keep WHO they are (headwear, hairstyle, facial hair, age, build, colors, weapon), but give them
adult body proportions and an adult face. No big head, no big round eyes, no button nose, no stubby limbs.

RANK: TRANSCENDENT —— cold silver dignity.
RANK LOOK IN ONE LINE: calm, cold and formal, a silver-white portrait in still air. At thumbnail size it must read "silver and pale sky-blue,
quiet and noble".

RANK RULES (checklist —— every panel must obey all of these):
- CAMERA AND POSE: eye-level, CHEST-TO-WAIST crop. The body faces the front or turns at most 30 degrees, shoulders level, the pose still and
  symmetrical, the weapon or item held VERTICALLY at one side (a tall quiet line). No diagonal action, no foreshortening, no wide-angle.
- PROPORTIONS AND RENDERING: stylized anime-style game illustration with ADULT proportions, about 5.5 to 6 heads tall (NOT chibi, NOT semi-chibi):
  a normal-size head on broad shoulders, adult faces with normal-size eyes, a drawn nose bridge, a defined jaw and cheekbones. Fine clean ink
  lines, soft cel shading with a little painted gradation and airy highlights. Every panel on the sheet uses the SAME proportions and rendering.
- OUTFIT: the base design made noble —— engraved SILVER and white-steel plates, silver filigree, pale silk over the faction-colored cloth
  (the cloth muted and a little desaturated), a white or pale-blue plume or tassels, plain silver clasps. NO GOLD anywhere.
- LIGHT: cold, bright and even, from the upper front-left, a thin white rim, soft pale blue-grey shadows. HIGH-KEY: the whole picture is light.
- COLOR: silver, white and pale sky-blue (#cfe6ff); the faction color only in the cloth. A cool temperature everywhere.
- BACKGROUND: pale blue-white mist fading to white at the bottom, and ONE tall faction war banner (blank cloth in the faction color, no lettering)
  standing far behind on one side, slightly out of focus. Nothing else: no crowd, no dramatic sky.
- EFFECTS: none, except a few tiny silver light motes. No fire, no lightning, no glowing aura.
- EXPRESSION: composed dignity, the eyes level, a restrained look.
- NEVER: chibi or super-deformed proportions, big heads, big round eyes, a jade ring or circle behind the head (that is the KIIN mark),
  gold or orange, fire or lightning, a dark background, a dynamic pose, crowds, a cape blowing in the wind.

GAME CROP SAFETY (the game cuts the face out of the top center of each picture for the card and for the small round battle medal):
- The whole HEAD (top of the hair or helmet down to the chin) sits in the TOP-CENTER zone: between 8% and 47% of the panel height, horizontally centered, never off to a side.
- Nothing important within 8% of the left and right edges or in the bottom 20% (the card cuts those). Plumes and flag tips may rise above the head.
- The face is always clearly lit and never covered by a hand, a weapon, a fan, hair or an effect.

EVERY CHARACTER LOOKS DIFFERENT: no two characters on a sheet share the same headgear, hairstyle or facial hair ——
follow each SIGNATURE line exactly (HEAD, FACIAL HAIR, MARK). All panels share the RANK LOOK (that is the point), but each person keeps their own
face, body, headwear and weapon, and their own pose within the rank's pose rule —— never clones in different clothes.
STRATEGISTS AND OFFICIALS ESPECIALLY: never default to "narrow slit eyes + long thin beard + tall black official hat" —— follow each FACE
and SIGNATURE exactly; their eye shapes (round, droopy, bulging, narrow), beards (none, stubble, goatee, short, long) and headwear must differ.
GENDER: every character is an adult MAN. Men read as men at a glance —— masculine jaw and brow, thicker neck, broad shoulders, male hairstyles and headwear; young men are young MEN, never girls: no long eyelashes, no blush, no rosy lips, no soft baby face.

LAYOUT:
- Image 1024x1536 portrait. ALWAYS a fixed grid of 2 columns x 2 rows = 4 equal slots, reading order left to right, top to bottom.
- Characters 1 to 4 fill the first 4 slots in order.
- Each slot holds ONE tall rounded-rectangle PANEL whose picture runs all the way to the panel edge: no inner border, no frame, no white margin.
- Natural proportions: never stretch, squash or shorten a character to fit a panel.
- Flat light green paper background (#9BCF88) around the panels, NARROW even gaps between them (about 3% of the image width).
  Nothing crosses into a neighbor: no plume, banner, weapon or effect leaves its own panel.
- Under each panel a dark green brush-stroke banner with ONLY its number (1 to 4) in big white digits.
- No other text anywhere: no names, no titles, no captions, no signature, no watermark. Do not draw the line labels as text.

CHARACTERS
1. Guan Ping  [SPEARMAN, Shu] —— same person as reference #1
   LOOK: early 20s, square jaw like his father but clean-shaven, ruddy (not deep red) face, earnest determined eyes.
   FACE: young MAN with a square honest face, ruddy cheeks, straight thick brows, wide steady eyes, firm closed mouth, clean-shaven, dutiful expression.
   BUILD: sturdy and square, medium height.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD green cloth headband with the hair in a tight topknot; FACIAL HAIR clean-shaven; MARK broad heavy saber, seal pouch at the belt.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
2. Pang De  [SPEARMAN, Wei] —— same person as reference #2
   LOOK: 40s, hard square jaw, full black beard, thick eyebrows, fierce unblinking eyes.
   FACE: hard square MAN's jaw, thick straight eyebrows, fierce unblinking eyes, broad nose, full black beard, tanned skin, grim determination.
   BUILD: solid and broad.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD blue cloth headscarf (no helmet); FACIAL HAIR full black beard; MARK small upright wooden coffin on a short rope behind his back, upright long spear.
   COLORS: Wei blue: navy, deep blue and steel with silver.
   STORY HINT (tiny and optional, only a faint shape in the mist or the thing he or she holds; the calm pose rule wins): marching out to face Guan Yu with his own coffin carried behind him, vowing to die or win.
3. Zhou Tai  [SPEARMAN, Wu] —— same person as reference #3
   LOOK: cropped hair, many scars and bandages, stoic expressionless face.
   FACE: expressionless stone-like face, cropped hair, many crisscrossing scars and bandages, dull steady eyes, flat nose, closed mouth, stoic guardian.
   BUILD: solid, broad, covered in bandages.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD cropped hair with bandages wrapped around the head; FACIAL HAIR no beard; MARK crisscrossing scars and bandages everywhere.
   COLORS: Wu red: crimson, red, orange and gold.
   STORY HINT (tiny and optional, only a faint shape in the mist or the thing he or she holds; the calm pose rule wins): the blood battle — shielding his lord with his own body, covered in wounds and arrows, unbroken.
4. Wen Chou  [CAVALRY, Independent] —— same person as reference #4
   LOOK: 30s, rough ugly face, thick drooping mustache, angry glare.
   FACE: rough lumpy face, thick brow ridge, angry bloodshot glare, flat broad nose, thick drooping mustache, scowling mouth.
   BUILD: big and brutish, broad shoulders.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD yellow-gold helmet with a tall red tassel; FACIAL HAIR thick drooping mustache, no beard; MARK long spear lowered for a charge, yellow-gold armor.
   COLORS: Yuan clan army colors: yellow and gold robes or armor with dark brown trims (the Yuan banner color).
   MOUNTED: riding a brown horse —— rider chest-to-waist facing nearly front; the horse's armored head and neck rise from the bottom edge in front of him, standing calmly.
