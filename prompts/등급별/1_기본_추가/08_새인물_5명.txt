A character design sheet of 5 Three Kingdoms (late Han dynasty China) generals for a mobile card game.
These are the BASE designs: every later, fancier version of each character will be copied from this sheet, so each
character must be clear, simple and instantly recognizable.

STYLE:
- Cute SD chibi, about 2.5 heads tall: big head, small body, short limbs, FULL BODY from head to feet, standing on the ground.
- Clean dark-brown ink outlines, flat soft colors with simple two-tone cel shading, light paper texture. Gentle and readable,
  like a hand-drawn sticker. Not glossy, not painterly, not detailed.
- Small calm-to-lively poses: standing, holding the signature weapon or item, a slight turn or a small gesture.
- Each character stands on a soft round blot of darker green watercolor (its panel), slightly bigger than the character.
  The blot is the only background: no scenery, no ground details, no props besides the character's own items.
- CAVALRY characters are ALWAYS on horseback (see MOUNTED), never standing on foot. Their panel may be a little wider.

OUTFIT LEVEL (BASE): plain practical clothes and simple armor in the character's colors —— cloth, leather, dull iron,
simple trims, no gold ornaments, no capes (unless the character's own DESIGN names a cape or cloak), no glowing effects. Iconic features (beards, eyepatch, headbands, signature
weapons, hats) are ALREADY present here, because later versions must keep them.

FACES AND SILHOUETTES MUST ALL DIFFER:
- Draw FACE exactly and exaggerate it a little, like a caricature: face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, facial hair, age.
- Follow BUILD: heights and body shapes really differ (giant, fat, tiny, hunched, slender). Ages really differ (smooth young
  vs. wrinkled old). Not everyone is handsome. No same-face syndrome, no generic cute face.
- The signature weapon or item and the hairstyle/headwear make each silhouette unique even as a black shape.
EVERY CHARACTER LOOKS DIFFERENT: on one sheet no two characters share the same headgear, hairstyle or facial hair ——
follow each SIGNATURE line exactly (HEAD, FACIAL HAIR, MARK). Same faction colors must never make them look like twins: vary
the armor shape, helmet, cape and silhouette. Only characters with a MOUNTED line ride a horse; everyone else is ON FOOT.
STRATEGISTS AND OFFICIALS ESPECIALLY: never default to "narrow slit eyes + long thin beard + tall black official hat" —— follow each FACE
and SIGNATURE exactly; their eye shapes (round, droopy, bulging, narrow), beards (none, stubble, goatee, short, long) and headwear must differ.
GENDER: every character on THIS sheet is an adult MAN. Men read as men at a glance ——
masculine jaw and brow, thicker neck, broad shoulders, male hairstyles and headwear. Young men are young MEN, never girls:
no long eyelashes, no blush, no rosy lips, no soft baby face.

ANATOMY: every character has exactly TWO arms and TWO hands, each holding at most one thing —— never draw an extra arm, an extra hand or a duplicated weapon.

LAYOUT:
- Image 1536x1024 landscape. ALWAYS a fixed grid of 5 columns x 1 rows = 5 equal slots, reading order left to right, top to bottom.
- Natural proportions in every slot: never stretch, squash or shorten a character to fit the slot; leave empty background instead.
- Characters 1 to 5 fill the first 5 slots in order.
- Flat light green paper background (#9BCF88) over the whole sheet. Wide even gaps between panels; nothing crosses into a neighbor.
- Under each panel a dark green brush-stroke banner with ONLY its number (1 to 5) in big white digits.
- No other text anywhere: no names, no titles, no captions, no signature, no watermark. Do not draw the line labels as text.

CHARACTERS
1. Yu Fan  [SORCERER, Wu]
   DESIGN: Yu Fan, blunt Wu scholar of the Book of Changes, casting yarrow stalks with glowing trigrams. On foot.
   LOOK: 60s, gaunt, wild grey hair, scowling brows, no cap.
   FACE: gaunt elderly face, wild grey hair, scowling brows, mouth open mid-rant.
   BUILD: gaunt old man.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD wild grey hair, no cap; FACIAL HAIR scowling, wispy grey beard; MARK yarrow stalks and glowing trigram slips.
   COLORS: Wu red: crimson, red, orange and gold.
2. Lu Fan  [SCHEMER, Wu]
   DESIGN: Lu Fan, Wu treasurer-schemer in rich crimson silk, counting gold on an abacus. On foot.
   LOOK: 40s, well-fed smiling face, neat mustache, rich silk cap.
   FACE: well-fed smiling face, neat mustache, shrewd narrow eyes, rosy cheeks.
   BUILD: well-fed, round.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD rich silk cap; FACIAL HAIR neat mustache; MARK abacus and a string of coins.
   COLORS: Wu red: crimson, red, orange and gold.
3. Gao Lan  [SPEARMAN, Independent]
   DESIGN: Gao Lan, heavy general of Yuan Shao with a long halberd and a tower shield. On foot.
   LOOK: 40s, wide flat face, short thick beard, heavy helmet and tower shield.
   FACE: stocky man, wide flat face, short thick beard, steady unimpressed eyes.
   BUILD: stocky.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD heavy helmet; FACIAL HAIR short thick beard; MARK long halberd and a tower shield.
   COLORS: his own colors as described (independent).
4. Huang Zu  [ARCHER, Independent]
   DESIGN: Huang Zu, Jiangxia commander whose archers shot Sun Jian, aiming from behind a river wall. On foot.
   LOOK: 50s, heavy jowls, droopy mustache, old bronze helmet.
   FACE: aging heavy-jowled face, droopy mustache, cruel narrowed eyes, sneer.
   BUILD: heavy-jowled, paunchy.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD old bronze helmet; FACIAL HAIR droopy mustache; MARK longbow, cruel narrowed eyes.
   COLORS: his own colors as described (independent).
5. Zhang Mancheng  [ROGUE, Independent]
   DESIGN: Zhang Mancheng, Yellow Turban rebel leader with a yellow headband and a curved blade. On foot.
   LOOK: 30s, rough peasant face, ragged beard, yellow headband.
   FACE: wild-eyed rough peasant face, yellow headband, ragged beard, shouting mouth.
   BUILD: rough, wiry.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD yellow headband; FACIAL HAIR ragged beard; MARK curved blade and a torch.
   COLORS: his own colors as described (independent).
