A character design sheet of 5 Three Kingdoms (late Han dynasty China) generals for a mobile card game.
These are the BASE designs: every later, fancier version of each character will be copied from this sheet, so each
character must be clear, simple and instantly recognizable.

STYLE:
- Cute SD chibi, about 2.5 heads tall: big head, small body, short limbs, FULL BODY from head to feet, standing on the ground.
- Clean dark-brown ink outlines, flat soft colors with simple two-tone cel shading, light paper texture. Gentle and readable,
  like a hand-drawn sticker. Not glossy, not painterly, not detailed.
- Small calm-to-lively poses: standing, holding the signature weapon or item, a slight turn or a small gesture.
- Each character stands on a soft round blot of darker green watercolor (its panel), slightly bigger than the character.
  The blot is the only background: no scenery, no ground details, no props besides the character's own items.
- CAVALRY characters are ALWAYS on horseback (see MOUNTED), never standing on foot. Their panel may be a little wider.

OUTFIT LEVEL (BASE): plain practical clothes and simple armor in the character's colors —— cloth, leather, dull iron,
simple trims, no gold ornaments, no capes (unless the character's own DESIGN names a cape or cloak), no glowing effects. Iconic features (beards, eyepatch, headbands, signature
weapons, hats) are ALREADY present here, because later versions must keep them.

FACES AND SILHOUETTES MUST ALL DIFFER:
- Draw FACE exactly and exaggerate it a little, like a caricature: face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, facial hair, age.
- Follow BUILD: heights and body shapes really differ (giant, fat, tiny, hunched, slender). Ages really differ (smooth young
  vs. wrinkled old). Not everyone is handsome. No same-face syndrome, no generic cute face.
- The signature weapon or item and the hairstyle/headwear make each silhouette unique even as a black shape.
EVERY CHARACTER LOOKS DIFFERENT: on one sheet no two characters share the same headgear, hairstyle or facial hair ——
follow each SIGNATURE line exactly (HEAD, FACIAL HAIR, MARK). Same faction colors must never make them look like twins: vary
the armor shape, helmet, cape and silhouette. Only characters with a MOUNTED line ride a horse; everyone else is ON FOOT.
STRATEGISTS AND OFFICIALS ESPECIALLY: never default to "narrow slit eyes + long thin beard + tall black official hat" —— follow each FACE
and SIGNATURE exactly; their eye shapes (round, droopy, bulging, narrow), beards (none, stubble, goatee, short, long) and headwear must differ.
GENDER: on THIS sheet characters 1, 2 and 4 (Huang Yueying, Sun Shangxiang, Cai Wenji) are adult WOMEN; characters 3 and 5 are adult MEN. Men read as men at a glance ——
masculine jaw and brow, thicker neck, broad shoulders, male hairstyles and headwear. Young men are young MEN, never girls:
no long eyelashes, no blush, no rosy lips, no soft baby face.

LAYOUT:
- Image 1536x1024 landscape. ALWAYS a fixed grid of 5 columns x 1 rows = 5 equal slots, reading order left to right, top to bottom.
- Natural proportions in every slot: never stretch, squash or shorten a character to fit the slot; leave empty background instead.
- Characters 1 to 5 fill the first 5 slots in order.
- Flat light green paper background (#9BCF88) over the whole sheet. Wide even gaps between panels; nothing crosses into a neighbor.
- Under each panel a dark green brush-stroke banner with ONLY its number (1 to 5) in big white digits.
- No other text anywhere: no names, no titles, no captions, no signature, no watermark. Do not draw the line labels as text.

CHARACTERS
1. Huang Yueying  [ARCHER, Shu]
   DESIGN: Huang Yueying, the clever wife of Zhuge Liang and an inventor, plain practical woman (not glamorous), on foot, holding a wooden repeating crossbow of her own design, small gears and tools hanging at the belt.
   LOOK: late 20s woman, plain face, freckles, sun-darkened skin, light yellow-brown hair in a thick practical braid.
   FACE: adult WOMAN with a plain broad face, freckles, sun-darkened skin, bright clever eyes, slightly crooked knowing smile.
   BUILD: short and sturdy woman.
   SIGNATURE (unique to her on this sheet): HEAD thick yellow-brown braid over one shoulder with a carpenter's pencil stuck in it; FACIAL HAIR none; MARK wooden repeating crossbow, belt of gears and tools.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
2. Sun Shangxiang  [ROGUE, Wu]
   DESIGN: Sun Shangxiang, the fierce martial princess of Wu who kept armed maids, short red battle robe over light armor, on foot, two short swords crossed, ready to spring.
   LOOK: about 20, bold grin, sharp bright eyes, hair in two high buns.
   FACE: adult young WOMAN with a heart-shaped face, bold toothy grin, sharp lively eyes, straight brows, fearless expression.
   BUILD: slim athletic woman, light on her feet.
   SIGNATURE (unique to her on this sheet): HEAD two high hair buns tied with red ribbons; FACIAL HAIR none; MARK two short swords crossed, short red battle robe.
   COLORS: Wu red: crimson, red, orange and gold.
3. Jiang Wan  [SCHOLAR, Shu]
   DESIGN: Jiang Wan, the calm steady minister who carried on the government of Zhuge Liang, plain green official robe, on foot, holding a thick ledger and an official seal.
   LOOK: 40s, calm round face, neat short beard, quiet reliable eyes.
   FACE: calm round face, gentle level eyes, soft eyebrows, short neat beard and mustache, small calm smile, steady trustworthy expression.
   BUILD: medium build, slightly soft, very steady stance.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD small square scholar's cap; FACIAL HAIR short neat beard and mustache; MARK thick ledger and official seal.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
4. Cai Wenji  [SCHOLAR, Wei]
   DESIGN: Cai Wenji, the great poet and musician who returned from the Xiongnu, Han robe with a nomad fur collar, on foot, holding a long nomad reed flute (hujia), a small guqin slung on her back.
   LOOK: early 30s woman, elegant but sorrowful, long black hair partly braided in nomad style.
   FACE: adult WOMAN with an oval face, sad gentle downturned eyes, thin arched brows, small closed mouth, melancholic dignified expression.
   BUILD: slender woman, tall.
   SIGNATURE (unique to her on this sheet): HEAD long black hair with thin nomad braids and a fur-trimmed cap; FACIAL HAIR none; MARK long reed flute, small guqin on her back, fur collar.
   COLORS: Wei blue: navy, deep blue and steel with silver, with a white nomad fur collar.
5. Nanhua Laoxian  [SORCERER, Independent]
   DESIGN: Nanhua Laoxian, the legendary old immortal who gave Zhang Jiao the Taiping book, white and pale green hermit robe, on foot, holding a glowing bamboo scroll, a gourd at the waist.
   LOOK: extremely old man, bald high domed forehead, very long white eyebrows hanging to his cheeks, white beard to the knees, kind mysterious smile.
   FACE: very old MAN with a huge bald domed forehead, long white eyebrows drooping past his cheeks, tiny twinkling eyes, round rosy nose, kind mysterious smile, white beard flowing to the knees.
   BUILD: small and round old man, short legs.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD completely bald high domed head; FACIAL HAIR white beard flowing down to the knees; MARK long drooping white eyebrows, glowing bamboo scroll, gourd.
   COLORS: white, pale green and pale gold (hermit).
