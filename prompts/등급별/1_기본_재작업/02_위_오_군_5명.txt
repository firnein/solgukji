A character design sheet of 5 Three Kingdoms (late Han dynasty China) generals for a mobile card game.
These are the BASE designs: every later, fancier version of each character will be copied from this sheet, so each
character must be clear, simple and instantly recognizable.

STYLE:
- Cute SD chibi, about 2.5 heads tall: big head, small body, short limbs, FULL BODY from head to feet, standing on the ground.
- Clean dark-brown ink outlines, flat soft colors with simple two-tone cel shading, light paper texture. Gentle and readable,
  like a hand-drawn sticker. Not glossy, not painterly, not detailed.
- Small calm-to-lively poses: standing, holding the signature weapon or item, a slight turn or a small gesture.
- Each character stands on a soft round blot of darker green watercolor (its panel), slightly bigger than the character.
  The blot is the only background: no scenery, no ground details, no props besides the character's own items.
- CAVALRY characters are ALWAYS on horseback (see MOUNTED), never standing on foot. Their panel may be a little wider.

OUTFIT LEVEL (BASE): plain practical clothes and simple armor in the character's colors —— cloth, leather, dull iron,
simple trims, no gold ornaments, no capes, no glowing effects. Iconic features (beards, eyepatch, headbands, signature
weapons, hats) are ALREADY present here, because later versions must keep them.

FACES AND SILHOUETTES MUST ALL DIFFER:
- Draw FACE exactly and exaggerate it a little, like a caricature: face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, facial hair, age.
- Follow BUILD: heights and body shapes really differ (giant, fat, tiny, hunched, slender). Ages really differ (smooth young
  vs. wrinkled old). Not everyone is handsome. No same-face syndrome, no generic cute face.
- The signature weapon or item and the hairstyle/headwear make each silhouette unique even as a black shape.
EVERY CHARACTER LOOKS DIFFERENT: on one sheet no two characters share the same headgear, hairstyle or facial hair ——
follow each SIGNATURE line exactly (HEAD, FACIAL HAIR, MARK). Same faction colors must never make them look like twins: vary
the armor shape, helmet, cape and silhouette. Only characters with a MOUNTED line ride a horse; everyone else is ON FOOT.
GENDER: every character is an adult MAN, except the women Diaochan, Da Qiao and Xiao Qiao. Men read as men at a glance ——
masculine jaw and brow, thicker neck, broad shoulders, male hairstyles and headwear. Young men are young MEN, never girls:
no long eyelashes, no blush, no rosy lips, no soft baby face.

LAYOUT:
- Image 1536x1024 landscape. ALWAYS a fixed grid of 5 columns x 1 rows = 5 equal slots, reading order left to right, top to bottom.
- Natural proportions in every slot: never stretch, squash or shorten a character to fit the slot; leave empty background instead.
- Characters 1 to 5 fill the first 5 slots in order.
- Flat light green paper background (#9BCF88) over the whole sheet. Wide even gaps between panels; nothing crosses into a neighbor.
- Under each panel a dark green brush-stroke banner with ONLY its number (1 to 5) in big white digits.
- No other text anywhere: no names, no titles, no captions, no signature, no watermark. Do not draw the line labels as text.

CHARACTERS
1. Guo Jia  [STRATEGIST, Wei]  ★ICONIC: draw the famous traditional look exactly
   DESIGN: Guo Jia, very young brilliant strategist who died young, slender and pale in a loose blue-grey robe, holding a SHORT CALLIGRAPHY BRUSH (no fan), a wine gourd at his hip
   LOOK: early 20s, smooth youthful face, clean-shaven.
   FACE: slender pale young MAN in his early 20s, smooth CLEAN-SHAVEN face (no beard, no stubble), angular sharp jaw, flushed sickly cheeks, sleepy half-lidded smug eyes, thin nose, crooked playful smirk, loose messy hair.
   BUILD: slender and loose-limbed.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD no hat, loose untied hair; FACIAL HAIR clean-shaven, no beard at all; MARK short calligraphy brush in hand, wine gourd, flushed cheeks.
   COLORS: Wei blue: navy, deep blue and steel with silver.
2. Lu Xun  [STRATEGIST, Wu]
   DESIGN: Lu Xun, young grand commander of Wu in a general's red-and-dark lamellar ARMOR, holding ONE straight commander's sword raised to direct the troops (a single sword, not two)
   LOOK: youthful man, red scholar's cap, very faint mustache line.
   FACE: young MAN with a smooth face but a defined jaw, calm narrow clever eyes, straight thin brows, small straight nose, composed closed mouth, very faint mustache line, fair skin, red scholar's cap.
   BUILD: slim but broad-shouldered.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD red scholar's cap; FACIAL HAIR very faint mustache line; MARK general's armor, ONE commander's sword raised.
   COLORS: Wu red: crimson, red, orange and gold.
3. Cao Ren  [SPEARMAN, Wei]
   DESIGN: Cao Ren, master of holding a fortress: CROUCHED in a guarding stance BEHIND a huge tower shield AS TALL AS A MAN, braced against it, only his helmeted head and fierce eyes showing over the top
   LOOK: square face with a broad greying beard, heavy round helmet with the visor raised.
   FACE: huge square head, small hard eyes under a heavy brow, wide nose, broad GREYING beard, ruddy skin, immovable defender.
   BUILD: massive and square, like a wall.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD heavy round helmet with the visor raised; FACIAL HAIR broad greying beard; MARK man-tall tower shield, crouched guarding stance.
   COLORS: Wei blue: navy, deep blue and steel with silver.
4. Yan Liang  [CAVALRY, Independent]
   DESIGN: Yan Liang, fierce general in an embroidered robe over gold armor, with a big blade.
   LOOK: 40s, broad fierce face, thick single unibrow, thick full black beard, gold-crested helmet.
   FACE: broad fierce MAN's face, thick single unibrow, small mean eyes, flat wide nose, thick full black beard, arrogant sneer.
   BUILD: big and brutish.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD gold-crested helmet; FACIAL HAIR thick full black beard; MARK embroidered robe over gold armor, big blade, unibrow.
   COLORS: Yuan clan army colors: yellow and gold robes or armor with dark brown trims (the Yuan banner color).
   MOUNTED: riding a big dark brown warhorse —— a small chubby chibi horse, rider AND whole horse visible from ears to hooves, both standing on the blot.
5. Chen Gong  [SCHEMER, Independent]
   DESIGN: Chen Gong, defiant strategist of Lü Bu captured at White Gate Tower: crimson-and-black robe, a plain black headscarf, a straight sword at his hip, a CUT ROPE still tied around one wrist, chin raised in proud defiance
   LOOK: 40s, lean proud face, neat long black beard, piercing stare, unbowed even as a captive.
   FACE: lean long face, prominent cheekbones, piercing defiant eyes, straight nose, thin firm lips, pale stern skin, proud raised chin.
   BUILD: lean and upright.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD plain black headscarf; FACIAL HAIR neat long black beard; MARK cut rope tied around one wrist, sword at the hip, crimson-black robe.
   COLORS: crimson red and black.
