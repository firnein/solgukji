A character design sheet of 5 Three Kingdoms (late Han dynasty China) generals for a mobile card game.
These are the BASE designs: every later, fancier version of each character will be copied from this sheet, so each
character must be clear, simple and instantly recognizable.

STYLE:
- Cute SD chibi, about 2.5 heads tall: big head, small body, short limbs, FULL BODY from head to feet, standing on the ground.
- Clean dark-brown ink outlines, flat soft colors with simple two-tone cel shading, light paper texture. Gentle and readable,
  like a hand-drawn sticker. Not glossy, not painterly, not detailed.
- Small calm-to-lively poses: standing, holding the signature weapon or item, a slight turn or a small gesture.
- Each character stands on a soft round blot of darker green watercolor (its panel), slightly bigger than the character.
  The blot is the only background: no scenery, no ground details, no props besides the character's own items.
- CAVALRY characters are ALWAYS on horseback (see MOUNTED), never standing on foot. Their panel may be a little wider.

OUTFIT LEVEL (BASE): plain practical clothes and simple armor in the character's colors —— cloth, leather, dull iron,
simple trims, no gold ornaments, no capes, no glowing effects. Iconic features (beards, eyepatch, headbands, signature
weapons, hats) are ALREADY present here, because later versions must keep them.

FACES AND SILHOUETTES MUST ALL DIFFER:
- Draw FACE exactly and exaggerate it a little, like a caricature: face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, facial hair, age.
- Follow BUILD: heights and body shapes really differ (giant, fat, tiny, hunched, slender). Ages really differ (smooth young
  vs. wrinkled old). Not everyone is handsome. No same-face syndrome, no generic cute face.
- The signature weapon or item and the hairstyle/headwear make each silhouette unique even as a black shape.
EVERY CHARACTER LOOKS DIFFERENT: on one sheet no two characters share the same headgear, hairstyle or facial hair ——
follow each SIGNATURE line exactly (HEAD, FACIAL HAIR, MARK). Same faction colors must never make them look like twins: vary
the armor shape, helmet, cape and silhouette. Only characters with a MOUNTED line ride a horse; everyone else is ON FOOT.
GENDER: every character is an adult MAN, except the women Diaochan, Da Qiao and Xiao Qiao. Men read as men at a glance ——
masculine jaw and brow, thicker neck, broad shoulders, male hairstyles and headwear. Young men are young MEN, never girls:
no long eyelashes, no blush, no rosy lips, no soft baby face.

LAYOUT:
- Image 1536x1024 landscape. ALWAYS a fixed grid of 5 columns x 1 rows = 5 equal slots, reading order left to right, top to bottom.
- Natural proportions in every slot: never stretch, squash or shorten a character to fit the slot; leave empty background instead.
- Characters 1 to 5 fill the first 5 slots in order.
- Flat light green paper background (#9BCF88) over the whole sheet. Wide even gaps between panels; nothing crosses into a neighbor.
- Under each panel a dark green brush-stroke banner with ONLY its number (1 to 5) in big white digits.
- No other text anywhere: no names, no titles, no captions, no signature, no watermark. Do not draw the line labels as text.

CHARACTERS
1. Guan Yu  [SPEARMAN, Shu]  ★ICONIC: draw the famous traditional look exactly
   DESIGN: Guan Yu, tall dignified general with a very long black beard and red face, green robe over armor, on foot, Green Dragon crescent glaive braced upright beside him.
   LOOK: deep red face, extremely long black beard, narrow phoenix eyes, green headscarf.
   FACE: long majestic face, deep date-red skin, narrow upturned phoenix eyes half-closed, silkworm eyebrows, straight nose, solemn closed mouth hidden in the beard, aloof proud expression.
   BUILD: very tall and dignified, long legs, upright.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD green cloth headscarf; FACIAL HAIR extremely long straight black beard reaching his belly; MARK deep date-red face, Green Dragon crescent glaive.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
2. Zhang Fei  [SPEARMAN, Shu]  ★ICONIC: draw the famous traditional look exactly
   DESIGN: Zhang Fei, wild bearded general with round glaring eyes, dark armor, on foot, serpent spear braced forward, shouting.
   LOOK: round glaring eyes, bristly tiger-whisker beard, thick eyebrows, dark tanned skin, wide mouth.
   FACE: wide round face, huge round bulging glaring eyes, bristly tiger-whisker beard bursting outward, thick black brows, flat wide nose, enormous roaring mouth, dark tanned skin.
   BUILD: big and burly, barrel chest.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD black cloth headwrap knotted at the brow over bristling wild hair; FACIAL HAIR bristly tiger-whisker beard bursting outward like spikes; MARK huge round bulging eyes, dark tanned skin, serpent-headed spear.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
3. Ma Dai  [CAVALRY, Shu]
   DESIGN: Ma Dai, loyal cousin of Ma Chao, green and bronze armor.
   LOOK: late 20s, round friendly face, short stubble beard, bronze helmet, loyal steady eyes.
   FACE: round friendly face, short stubble beard, loyal round eyes, broad nose, earnest smile, tanned frontier skin.
   BUILD: sturdy.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD round bronze helmet without plume; FACIAL HAIR short stubble beard; MARK round friendly face, bronze-trimmed armor.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
   MOUNTED: riding a bay horse —— a small chubby chibi horse, rider AND whole horse visible from ears to hooves, both standing on the blot.
4. Ma Chao  [CAVALRY, Shu]  ★ICONIC: draw the famous traditional look exactly
   DESIGN: Ma Chao, dashing young general in shining silver lion-head armor with a green cape.
   LOOK: early 20s young man, narrow jade-white face, fierce slanted shooting-star eyes, thin sharp mustache, scowl, silver lion-head helmet with a tall white plume.
   FACE: young MAN with a narrow jade-white face and sharp cheekbones, fierce upturned shooting-star eyes under slanted brows, thin straight nose, scowling mouth showing a fang, thin sharp mustache, cold vengeful warrior.
   BUILD: slim, lean and sharp, wiry.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD silver lion-head helmet with a tall white plume; FACIAL HAIR thin sharp mustache; MARK jade-white face, white mourning robe over silver lion armor, long spear, black horse.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
   MOUNTED: riding a black Xiliang warhorse —— a small chubby chibi horse, rider AND whole horse visible from ears to hooves, both standing on the blot.
5. Zhao Yun  [CAVALRY, Shu]  ★ICONIC: draw the famous traditional look exactly
   DESIGN: Zhao Yun, handsome heroic general in white-silver armor with green trim, silver spear.
   LOOK: late 20s, broad face with a full heavy jaw, thick brows, large calm eyes, clean-shaven, NO helmet, neat topknot with a white-and-green headband, gentle confident smile.
   FACE: heroic MAN with a broad face and a full heavy jaw, thick dark brows, large clear calm eyes, straight nose, gentle confident closed-mouth smile, clean-shaven, healthy tanned skin, warm honorable expression.
   BUILD: tall, broad-chested and solid.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD no helmet, neat topknot tied with a white-and-green headband; FACIAL HAIR clean-shaven; MARK white and green robe over light silver armor, dragon-engraved silver spear, white horse.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
   MOUNTED: riding a pure white horse with a green saddle cloth —— a small chubby chibi horse, rider AND whole horse visible from ears to hooves, both standing on the blot.
