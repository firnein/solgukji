A character design sheet of 5 Three Kingdoms (late Han dynasty China) generals for a mobile card game.
These are the BASE designs: every later, fancier version of each character will be copied from this sheet, so each
character must be clear, simple and instantly recognizable.

STYLE:
- Cute SD chibi, about 2.5 heads tall: big head, small body, short limbs, FULL BODY from head to feet, standing on the ground.
- Clean dark-brown ink outlines, flat soft colors with simple two-tone cel shading, light paper texture. Gentle and readable,
  like a hand-drawn sticker. Not glossy, not painterly, not detailed.
- Small calm-to-lively poses: standing, holding the signature weapon or item, a slight turn or a small gesture.
- Each character stands on a soft round blot of darker green watercolor (its panel), slightly bigger than the character.
  The blot is the only background: no scenery, no ground details, no props besides the character's own items.
- CAVALRY characters are ALWAYS on horseback (see MOUNTED), never standing on foot. Their panel may be a little wider.

OUTFIT LEVEL (BASE): plain practical clothes and simple armor in the character's colors —— cloth, leather, dull iron,
simple trims, no gold ornaments, no capes, no glowing effects. Iconic features (beards, eyepatch, headbands, signature
weapons, hats) are ALREADY present here, because later versions must keep them.

FACES AND SILHOUETTES MUST ALL DIFFER:
- Draw FACE exactly and exaggerate it a little, like a caricature: face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, facial hair, age.
- Follow BUILD: heights and body shapes really differ (giant, fat, tiny, hunched, slender). Ages really differ (smooth young
  vs. wrinkled old). Not everyone is handsome. No same-face syndrome, no generic cute face.
- The signature weapon or item and the hairstyle/headwear make each silhouette unique even as a black shape.
EVERY CHARACTER LOOKS DIFFERENT: on one sheet no two characters share the same headgear, hairstyle or facial hair ——
follow each SIGNATURE line exactly (HEAD, FACIAL HAIR, MARK). Same faction colors must never make them look like twins: vary
the armor shape, helmet, cape and silhouette. Only characters with a MOUNTED line ride a horse; everyone else is ON FOOT.
GENDER: every character is an adult MAN, except the women Diaochan, Da Qiao and Xiao Qiao. Men read as men at a glance ——
masculine jaw and brow, thicker neck, broad shoulders, male hairstyles and headwear. Young men are young MEN, never girls:
no long eyelashes, no blush, no rosy lips, no soft baby face.

LAYOUT:
- Image 1536x1024 landscape. ALWAYS a fixed grid of 5 columns x 1 rows = 5 equal slots, reading order left to right, top to bottom.
- Natural proportions in every slot: never stretch, squash or shorten a character to fit the slot; leave empty background instead.
- Characters 1 to 5 fill the first 5 slots in order.
- Flat light green paper background (#9BCF88) over the whole sheet. Wide even gaps between panels; nothing crosses into a neighbor.
- Under each panel a dark green brush-stroke banner with ONLY its number (1 to 5) in big white digits.
- No other text anywhere: no names, no titles, no captions, no signature, no watermark. Do not draw the line labels as text.

CHARACTERS
1. Huang Zhong  [ARCHER, Shu]  ★ICONIC: draw the famous traditional look exactly
   DESIGN: Huang Zhong, white-bearded old veteran in green armor, drawing a huge longbow with a fierce grin, quiver on back.
   LOOK: 60s, white beard and white bushy eyebrows, wrinkles, muscular old arms.
   FACE: long weathered face, sunken cheeks, small fierce squinting eyes in deep wrinkles, big hawk nose, wide grinning mouth with a missing tooth, leathery tanned skin.
   BUILD: lean old body with sinewy muscular arms.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD green bandana tied low over a white topknot; FACIAL HAIR long white beard and big white bushy eyebrows; MARK deep wrinkles, huge longbow.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
2. Liao Hua  [ROGUE, Shu]
   DESIGN: Liao Hua, former Yellow Turban scout with a faded yellow headband, light leather armor, holding two daggers, alert pose.
   LOOK: thin wiry body, faded yellow headband, scar across the nose.
   FACE: thin wiry face, alert darting eyes, scar across the nose, faded yellow headband, tight cautious mouth, sun-weathered skin.
   BUILD: thin and wiry.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD faded yellow headband; FACIAL HAIR clean-shaven thin face; MARK scar across the nose, two daggers.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
3. Wei Yan  [ROGUE, Shu]  ★ICONIC: draw the famous traditional look exactly
   DESIGN: Wei Yan, the classic fierce rebel-boned general: deep date-red face, star-like eyes, an iron demon mask pushed up on his head, dark-green armor, swinging a huge two-handed broad-bladed saber. Rogue hint only: a light, agile, low crouching pose.
   LOOK: 40s, deep date-red face, glaring star-like eyes, thin twisted mustache, bare chin, iron demon mask pushed up on his head, bony bump at the BACK of the head, sneer.
   FACE: MAN with a deep date-red face, bright star-like glaring eyes, hooked nose, thin twisted mustache and a bare chin, sneering mouth, a visible bony bump at the BACK of his skull.
   BUILD: medium height, broad, hunched forward like a wolf.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD black iron demon mask pushed up on top of his head, topknot, bony bump at the BACK of the skull; FACIAL HAIR thin twisted mustache, bare chin; MARK deep date-red face, dark-green spiked armor, huge two-handed broad-bladed saber.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
4. Zhou Cang  [ROGUE, Shu]
   DESIGN: Zhou Cang, rough loyal bandit-turned-retainer with a big beard, bare arms, light cloth armor, holding Guan Yu's Green Dragon glaive upright for his lord.
   LOOK: tall giant, near-black skin, wild curly hair, huge curly black beard down to his chest, very muscular, honest loyal face.
   FACE: MAN with very dark, almost black skin, huge thick CURLY black beard down to his chest, big round loyal eyes, broad flat nose, honest open mouth, wild curly hair.
   BUILD: tall dark-skinned giant, thick arms.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD bare head, wild curly black hair; FACIAL HAIR huge curly black beard down to his chest; MARK near-black skin, bare muscular arms, carrying Guan Yu's Green Dragon glaive upright like an attendant.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
5. Jiang Wei  [STRATEGIST, Shu]  ★ICONIC: draw the famous traditional look exactly
   DESIGN: Jiang Wei, the classic heir of Zhuge Liang: young earnest general in green armor holding his three-pointed spear. Strategist hint only: a white feather fan tucked at his waist.
   LOOK: early 20s young man, short helmet with green plume, short sideburns.
   FACE: young MAN in his early 20s, firm square jaw, strong straight thick brows, keen earnest eyes, straight nose, determined closed mouth, short sideburns and a faint stubble shadow on the chin, sun-tanned skin, broad neck, serious soldier's look.
   BUILD: athletic young soldier.
   SIGNATURE (unique to him on this sheet): HEAD short helmet with a green plume; FACIAL HAIR short sideburns and a faint chin stubble; MARK three-pointed spear.
   COLORS: Shu green: green, jade and earthy gold.
