An animation sprite sheet of ONE generic Three Kingdoms (late Han dynasty China) soldier for a mobile strategy game.
The same soldier is drawn 8 times in 8 poses. These sprites will stand on a 3D battlefield, so every pose must match exactly
in size, proportions, colors and costume — only the pose changes.

STYLE (must match our general portraits):
- Cute SD chibi, about 2.5 heads tall: big head, small body, short limbs, FULL BODY from head to feet.
- Clean dark-brown ink outlines, flat soft colors with simple two-tone cel shading. Readable as a tiny figure: strong silhouette,
  few small details. Not glossy, not painterly, no gradients, no glow.
- A plain anonymous rank-and-file soldier: simple face, no famous-general features, no beard unless stated.

VIEW: pure SIDE VIEW, the soldier faces RIGHT in every pose (looking and moving toward the right edge). No front or back views.

TEAM COLOR (very important): the parts listed under TEAM PARTS are painted in ONE flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) with only a slightly
darker magenta for shadow. The game recolors magenta into each army's color. Use magenta NOWHERE else, and never use pink,
purple or red-violet anywhere else on the soldier.
NO GREEN anywhere on the soldier (no green cloth, trims or items) —— green is the background color and gets erased.

LAYOUT:
- Image 2048x1024 landscape. A fixed grid of 4 columns x 2 rows = 8 equal square cells, reading order left to right, top to bottom.
- Exactly one pose per cell, centered, never crossing into a neighbor cell. Leave a wide empty margin around every pose.
- The soldier is the SAME SIZE in every cell, and his feet stand on the same invisible ground line near the bottom of each cell
  (except pose 7, which lies on that ground line).
- Flat light green background (#9BCF88) over the whole sheet. No ground, no shadows, no scenery, no effects, no dust,
  no motion lines, no grid lines, no borders.
- No text anywhere: no numbers, no labels, no captions, no signature, no watermark.

POSES (cell 1 to 8):
1. Standing ready, weapon held in the normal ready position.
2. Walking: left foot forward, right foot back, mid-stride.
3. Walking: right foot forward, left foot back, mid-stride (the opposite step of pose 2).
4. Attack wind-up: body coiled back, weapon drawn back ready to strike.
5. Attack strike: lunging forward to the right, weapon fully extended or swung through toward the right.
6. Hit: flinching backward (leaning left), eyes squeezed shut, weapon still in hand.
7. Fallen: lying flat on his back on the ground line, head toward the left, weapon dropped beside him.
8. Cheering: standing, weapon raised high above his head in victory.

SOLDIER: SORCERER (Taoist mystic)  —— 병종 술사
- Loose Taoist robe, a dark pointed hood, a cord belt with a small gourd; older, thin beard.
- ITEM: a tall wooden staff with a small glowing-orange orb on top (the orb is orange, not magenta). Pose 4 raises the staff, pose 5 points it forward to the right.
TEAM PARTS (magenta #FF00FF): the robe body and sleeves.
Everything else uses natural dull colors (iron grey, leather brown, dark cloth, skin).
