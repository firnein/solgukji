# 전투 원화 시안 01

image_gen 내장 도구로 만든 미술 방향 시안이며 게임 구현 화면이 아니다.

같은 전장의 대치 → 충돌 → 붕괴를 비교한다. 대열의 실루엣, 전선의 변형, 연결되는 화공, 절제된 정보 표시가 목적이다. 병사·부대 수와 시간·공격 경로는 실제 엔진의 검증값이 아니다.

화공은 출처가 보이지만 긴 불길이 광선처럼 보일 수 있어 구현에서는 발사체 이동 뒤 대상 대열을 따라 번지게 다듬을 필요가 있다. 휴대폰에서의 장수 식별은 다음 구현 시안에서 검증해야 한다.

## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: ONE cohesive art-direction storyboard sheet for a new Three Kingdoms mobile auto-battler, containing THREE tall portrait gameplay screens side by side, not three separate files. Render a polished concept illustration, not an actual screenshot or an implementation claim. Start from a blank creative direction: do not imitate a generic isometric RTS screenshot.

Primary request: A living illustrated war painting in which whole military formations are the characters. The user dislikes dense tiny noisy soldier crowds and excessive Dynasty Warriors-like soldiers popping into the air. Convey army scale through ranks, spear silhouettes, banners and depth, while making each unit and its battlefield role instantly legible on a small phone.

Sheet layout: wide landscape approximately 3:2. Three equal 9:16 portrait screens with thin warm parchment gutters, no device hardware. Above each screen a restrained Korean heading, exactly "대치", "충돌", "붕괴". Same camera, terrain, commanders, unit identities, faction colors, and UI in all three panels: successive moments of ONE battle, not three alternate environments.

Art direction: premium hand-painted 2.5D historical strategy illustration, expressive brush edges and crisp readable shapes, aged paper and mineral pigment, subdued moss and warm earth terrain, dark ink outlines used selectively. Graphic, designed, playable-looking composition rather than photorealism, chibi figures, tiny pixel speckles or ornate fantasy. Army blue is muted indigo; army red is deep cinnabar; fire is the one luminous orange accent. Restrained brass on flags only, no gold sparkly units. Each formation has a clear occupied shape with large breathing spaces separating formations; a few well drawn recognizable leading soldiers plus simplified rear ranks imply many troops. Faces not necessary on individual tiny soldiers. Substantial visible empty terrain is essential.

Camera: fixed high oblique view from above, all battlefield within each portrait screen. Allied indigo army in lower half facing upward, cinnabar enemy army in upper half facing downward. Terrain contains a recognizable shallow winding river across mid-field, a stone crossing in the center, small distant treeline framing the sides, flat central open ground. Do not cover battlefield in UI or floating commander circles. Nine unit formations on each side, grouped naturally as front line, middle and rear support. Infantry should read as shield and spear WALLS, cavalry as a horse-and-banner WEDGE, archers as SPACED bow lines, strategists as small command groups with cloth standards. Formation silhouettes more important than counting individual soldiers. Commanders belong to formations via a distinctive taller banner and a lead figure, not oversized floating face tokens.

Panel 1 "대치": visibly composed opposing armies, disciplined intact ranks, central blue cavalry wedge poised behind blue shield line, blue archers in rear, a blue fire strategist on lower right carrying a small white-and-indigo standard. Enemy has central shield line and archers at upper right. Nobody is airborne. Sparse quiet scene with tension and room to understand the formations.
Panel 2 "충돌": same locations, camera and terrain. Central infantry front lines meet at the crossing: shields lean, spears brace, restrained contact dust. Blue cavalry wedge begins pressing through a small central opening, enemy shield front visibly buckles. On right flank a clear continuous curved strip of orange fire travels from the allied fire-strategist's direction into the enemy right infantry and toward enemy archers. Two or three connected tongues of flame communicate spread, not a screen-filling explosion, not laser lines or floating words. Archers release one clean readable volley. All soldiers on the ground; no flying bodies, large damage numbers, comic hit text or starbursts.
Panel 3 "붕괴": same battle a few seconds later. Enemy central shield formation is BROKEN into two separated clusters with an unmistakable empty breach; blue cavalry has pushed through the breach as an organized wedge. One enemy banner droops or falls and the defeated cluster retreats upward with backs turned. Fire has spread across the enemy right rear formation, not the whole screen. Left and allied rear formations stay orderly, showing contrast. Defeat is communicated by a broken front and retreat, not by dozens of corpses or gore. No airborne humans or scattered popcorn fragments.

Interface: redesign from scratch to be quiet. A thin top ink-colored band per screen with two compact blue/red strength bars and a small centered phase/time indicator; bottom narrow dark parchment command strip with NINE small handsome painted Three Kingdoms commander portrait thumbnails arranged as one restrained 5+4 two-row roster, a few tiny readiness bars, no textual stat blocks. Same commander portraits across all screens. No big character portrait cut-in. No big names over every unit, no damage-number soup, no menus covering battlefield. No modern sci-fi icons, glossy trading card borders, large circular portrait markers, hex grids or green health bars over every soldier. Only the three Korean headings need legible text. The art should clearly show how a quiet, legible combat theater can replace a noisy swarm renderer.
